Meet the band

Matched twelve-second clips of Faded Love and a quicker Ida Red, with the same notes, tempo, mix controls and dry room. Compare the previous patches with the new sound candidate. Levels have not been normalized.

The new candidate blends short fiddle strokes into sustained bow tone as notes lengthen, avoiding the old abrupt switch at 200 ms. The vintage rhythm guitar now uses microphone-recorded archtop plucks; the kit gains real acoustic kick and closed-hat hits. Saw Fiddle retains one saw, tracked high/low filters, a 60 ms held-note attack and delayed vibrato. Steel, upright bass, piano and clarinet are unchanged references.

Acoustic fiddle, piano, bass, snare and clarinet sources: VSCO2 Community Edition, CC0. Recorded by Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell; sample cutting by Elan Hickler / Soundemote. A modern acoustic source shaped for this instrument; no exact historical instrument/take match is claimed. Archtop sources: Shinyguitar, D. Smolken / Karoryfer Samples. Kick and closed hat: Virtuosity Drums, Versilian Studios and Karoryfer Samples, performed by Austin McMahon. Both CC0. Steel and the electronic layers use original synthesis. This is a listening candidate; physical FM-1 listening and deadlines remain untested.
