Back to the bandstand

Welcome to the Dance Hall

You’re Bob. Bring the players in, call a steel break, and take the band from the ballroom to the dance floor. R36 opens on DANCE HALL. Press PLAY to start.

Your first tune

Press EDIT for the Song Book. Turn PRESETS to browse the 50 tunes and press PLAY to choose. While the band is playing on the Dance Hall, turn PRESETS to queue the next tune at the next bar. Press REC to generate an original Western swing song.

Leave the controls alone for eight seconds and the show takes the screen. A cue gives the show its screen back after 2.5 seconds. Touch a control to bring the controls back. The song, chord, section and beat lamps keep your place.

Lead the band

The first eight cue keys on the Dance Hall give these directions, from left to right. The other keys play your selected instrument.

Then, Now and Funk

ALGORITHM moves between THEN and NOW from every page. LFO jumps straight between the two ends. The large readout appears when you change it. Now brings the electronic band into view and changes the camera style.

Press SEL for BAND. Its first eight cue keys switch the individual band players on or off. A muted player leaves the stage. Turn KNOB 3 for FUNK. The dancers follow the change.

Watch the show

The road, ballroom and radio sets follow the song. Bridges bring the camera closer to the rhythm section. Bob reacts to your directions. THEN uses broadcast-style iris transitions. NOW cuts like music television, with a featured player in the picture-in-picture window.

A tune’s ending lights the applause sign. When a tune finishes, press PLAY within the eight-second encore window to bring the band back with Bob’s holler. Stopping closes the curtain.
